# Dubai Parks and Resorts - Brand Story and Tone of Voice

## 1) Brand Story

Dubai Parks and Resorts is positioned as a complete entertainment destination where families, friends, and visitors can turn a regular day into a high-energy shared memory. The brand brings multiple worlds together in one place: cinematic thrills, water-based play, football culture, indoor adventure, themed dining, retail discovery, and on-site stays.

At its core, the brand promise is simple: make planning easy and make experiences unforgettable. Guests should feel they can arrive with different interests and still find something everyone is excited about, from first-time young visitors to returning thrill-seekers.

The story the brand tells is not only about rides. It is about togetherness, celebration, and discovery at every step of the journey:
- Before arrival: anticipation, easy choices, clear value.
- During the visit: immersion, joy, comfort, and variety.
- After the visit: memorable moments worth repeating and sharing.

The emotional territory is optimism, wonder, and connection. The practical territory is convenience, clarity, and confidence.

### Brand Essence
One destination, many worlds, shared joy.

### Brand Pillars
- Variety at scale: multiple parks and experiences for different interests.
- Family-first inclusivity: options for children, teens, adults, and groups.
- Memorable immersion: story-led attractions and themed environments.
- Seamless guest journey: straightforward planning, ticketing, and stay options.
- Repeat-worthy value: reasons to return across seasons and occasions.

### Audience Segments
- Families in the UAE and GCC planning leisure days or staycations.
- International tourists seeking marquee Dubai experiences.
- Youth and thrill-oriented groups.
- Occasion-based visitors (birthdays, school trips, celebrations, corporate groups).

### Value Proposition
A destination experience that combines world-class attractions, hospitality, and convenience in one integrated place.

## 2) Tone of Voice

### Voice Attributes
- Exciting: energetic and forward-moving.
- Warm: welcoming, human, and inclusive.
- Confident: clear and decisive without overclaiming.
- Helpful: practical and action-oriented.
- Imaginative: vivid language that sparks curiosity.

### Voice Formula
Use this balance in most content:
- 40% inspiration (emotion, excitement, storytelling)
- 40% clarity (facts, what is included, what to do next)
- 20% urgency (timely prompts, seasonal momentum)

### Writing Guidelines
- Lead with the guest benefit in the first sentence.
- Keep sentences short to medium length.
- Prefer active voice and present tense.
- Use specific nouns over hype words.
- Avoid jargon, slang overload, or forced humor.
- Keep calls-to-action direct and low-friction.

### Vocabulary Direction
Preferred words:
- discover, explore, celebrate, unforgettable, family, adventure, thrills, splash, stay, plan, book, today, now

Use sparingly:
- best-ever, ultimate, unbelievable, once-in-a-lifetime

Avoid:
- complex internal terms, negative framing, hard-sell pressure language

### Messaging by Funnel Stage
Awareness:
- Goal: spark curiosity and destination appeal.
- Style: cinematic, broad, emotionally rich.

Consideration:
- Goal: reduce planning friction.
- Style: clearer detail, practical comparisons, confidence-building.

Conversion:
- Goal: drive booking action.
- Style: concise value, clear inclusions, urgent but respectful CTA.

Post-visit/Retention:
- Goal: encourage return visits and loyalty.
- Style: celebratory recap, what to do next, seasonal discovery.

## 3) Channel-Specific Tone

Website homepage:
- Big, inspiring language with immediate wayfinding.

Product or ticket pages:
- Crisp, specific, and transparent.

Email or CRM:
- Personalized, useful, and time-aware.

Social:
- Lively, visual-first, community-oriented.

Support content:
- Calm, precise, empathetic, and solution-first.

## 4) Do and Do Not

Do:
- Make each message feel like an invitation.
- Explain value in plain language.
- Keep CTAs explicit: Book now, Explore parks, Plan your visit.
- Balance excitement with practical details.

Do not:
- Overpromise outcomes.
- Hide key conditions behind vague wording.
- Use inconsistent capitalization or naming for parks/products.
- Write generic tourism copy that could belong to any destination.

## 5) Agent Ingestion Snippet

Use the following as system-style instruction text for content agents:

"You are writing for Dubai Parks and Resorts. Keep the brand voice exciting, warm, confident, helpful, and imaginative. Always lead with guest value, then provide clear details and a direct next step. Write in active voice, short-to-medium sentences, and plain language. Balance inspiration with utility: 40% emotional excitement, 40% clarity, 20% urgency. Avoid overhype, vague claims, and internal jargon. Prioritize family-friendly inclusivity and destination-scale variety. Ensure naming consistency across parks, attractions, hotels, and ticket products."

## 6) Optional Prompt Add-On for Product Feed Descriptions

"When generating product names, descriptions, and keywords for feed items, emphasize experience type, audience fit (family, thrill, kids, group), and practical intent (day pass, annual pass, stay, attraction). Keep descriptions concrete, positive, and scan-friendly."
